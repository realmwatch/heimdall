Before you save one

  • Your key is stored encrypted, and only used to start the hand-offs you choose to run as you, and when you press Test. Admins can't see it or use it from Heimdall.
  • Anyone with root on the hub could still decrypt and use it, as they could the service account's. If the hub is ever compromised, revoke the key in Amp and make a new one.
  • You can remove it here at any time; it's also removed if your Heimdall account is disabled or deleted.
